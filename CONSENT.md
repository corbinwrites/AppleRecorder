# Recording consent

Apple Recorder records audio from your microphone and from apps you choose. **You** are the person making the recording.

- Recording laws differ by place. California Penal Code section 632 makes it a crime to record a confidential communication without the consent of all parties. Many other states and countries have similar rules.
- Room recordings capture people who never see this app. Tell everyone present before you press Record.
- Check the terms of any service you record (for example Discord).
- This document is not legal advice.

The app asks you to acknowledge this before the first recording.
